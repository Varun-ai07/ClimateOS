Source: Coimbatore Municipal Corporation — City Development Plan 2024
Topic: Urban infrastructure and flood vulnerability

City profile:
- Population: 1.6 million (2024), growing at 2.8% annually
- Area: 246 sq km, elevation 411-440m ASL
- Major waterways: Noyyal River (south), Siruvani River (west)
- Historical floods: 2018 (Noyyal overflow), 2019 (urban flash floods), 2023 (monsoon flooding)
- Drainage system: 340 km of storm water drains, 65% constructed before 2000
- Current drainage capacity: handles 55mm/hr peak intensity (design standard: 60mm/hr)
- Health infrastructure: 12 hospitals, 45 primary health centres, 3 in high-risk flood zones
- Schools in flood zones: 8 schools with >500 students each in Zone A and Zone B

Vulnerability factors:
- Rapid urbanization reducing natural drainage in western corridors
- Encroachment on Noyyal River floodplain in Zone A
- Aging drainage infrastructure with 30% blockage/siltation
- Limited real-time flood monitoring (only 3 gauge stations for entire city)
